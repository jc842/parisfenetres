# Haussmannian Window Replacement in Paris: Heritage Rules and ABF Approvals

Renovating windows in iconic 19th-century Parisian Haussmannian buildings demands an uncompromising balance between architectural preservation and modern insulating performance.

## The Role of the Architectes des Bâtiments de France (ABF)

Most Parisian arrondissements fall under protected historical zones. Replacing outward-facing facade windows requires prior municipal authorization (*déclaration préalable de travaux*) and ABF sign-off.

### Mandatory Preservation Criteria
- **Material Authenticity**: Wood (oak or larch) is almost universally required for street facades; PVC is systematically rejected by heritage authorities.
- **Traditional Profiling**: Narrow central stiles (*mouton et gueule de loup* opening mechanisms), authentic cremone bolts (*crémones en applique*), and period espagnolettes.
- **Transoms and Glazing Bars**: Preservation of original proportions, arched transoms, and decorative exterior moldings.

By combining double-glazed insulating units with traditional joinery techniques, owners achieve contemporary acoustic quiet without sacrificing historic prestige.
